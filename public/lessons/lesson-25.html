欢迎进入 Stage 5：计算机动画与物理仿真！在电影运镜与游戏过场动画中，我们通常用贝塞尔曲线或样条曲线 $\mathbf{P}(u)$ 作为摄像机导轨。但几乎所有初学者都会踩中两个经典的几何陷阱： 第一，明明在代码里让曲线参数 $u(t) = t$ 随时间均匀增加，摄像机却在控制点密集处像蜗牛一样卡顿，在控制点稀疏处像失控过山车一样暴冲！ 第二，如果直接用微分几何的 Frenet–Serret 标架 ($\mathbf{T}, \mathbf{N}, \mathbf{B}$) 控制镜头朝向，一旦飞船穿过 S 型轨道的拐点 (Inflection Point)，整个屏幕画面会在一帧之内瞬间上下颠倒 $180^\circ$！本课我们将用微积分链式法则与微分几何彻底解开这两个谜团。 Welcome to Stage 5: Computer Animation & Physical Simulation! When driving a camera along a spline $\mathbf{P}(u)$, two classic geometric traps await: First, stepping the curve parameter uniformly ($u(t)=t$) makes the camera crawl where control points are bunched and surge wildly where they are stretched! Second, aligning the camera with the Frenet–Serret frame ($\mathbf{T}, \mathbf{N}, \mathbf{B}$) causes an instant $180^\circ$ upside-down flip whenever the path crosses an inflection point! Let's derive why and build the exact mathematical cures.

链式法则剖析参数速度失控 & 弧长参数化反函数抵消定理 Chain Rule Analysis of Parametric Speed & Arc-Length Inversion Cancellation Theorem

UC Davis ECS 175 L20：如何让 $\frac{du}{ds} = \frac{1}{\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|}$ 完美约掉曲线自身的导数模长波动？ UC Davis ECS 175 L20: How $\frac{du}{ds} = \frac{1}{\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|}$ cancels out intrinsic curve stretching

步骤 A：为什么 $u(t)=t$ 的物理速率不恒定？ Step A: Why $u(t)=t$ Has Non-Uniform Physical Speed

设样条曲线为 $\mathbf{P}(u)$，$u \in [0,1]$。相机在三维世界中的瞬时物理速度向量为 $\mathbf{v}(t) = \frac{d\mathbf{P}}{dt}$。由微积分链式法则 (Chain Rule)： Let the spline be $\mathbf{P}(u)$, $u \in [0,1]$. The camera's 3D velocity is $\mathbf{v}(t) = \frac{d\mathbf{P}}{dt}$. By the Chain Rule:

$$\left\|\frac{d\mathbf{P}}{dt}\right\| = \left\|\frac{d\mathbf{P}}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dt}\right\| = \underbrace{\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|}_{\text{曲线内禀拉伸率}} \cdot \frac{du}{dt}$$

对于三次多项式曲线 $\mathbf{P}(u) = \sum_{i=0}^3 \mathbf{c}_i u^i$，导数模长 $\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\| = \sqrt{x'(u)^2 + y'(u)^2 + z'(u)^2}$ 随 $u$ 剧烈变化！若令 $\frac{du}{dt} = 1$，物理速率就会完全等于 $\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|$ 而忽快忽慢。 For a cubic polynomial curve, $\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\| = \sqrt{x'(u)^2 + y'(u)^2 + z'(u)^2}$ fluctuates wildly with $u$. Setting $\frac{du}{dt}=1$ leaves the physical speed at the mercy of $\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|$.

步骤 B：弧长积分 $s(u)$ 与反函数导数完美约分 Step B: Arc-Length Integral $s(u)$ & Inverse Derivative Cancellation

定义从 $0$ 到 $u$ 的累计弧长函数 $s(u) = \int_0^u \|\mathbf{P}'(\tau)\|\,d\tau$。由微积分基本定理，$\frac{ds}{du} = \|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|$。 因为 $s(u)$ 单调递增，反解出反函数 $u = s^{-1}(s)$。由反函数求导定理，若我们按目标弧长 $s(t)$ 推进，则： Define cumulative arc length $s(u) = \int_0^u \|\mathbf{P}'(\tau)\|\,d\tau$, so $\frac{ds}{du} = \|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|$. Inverting $s(u)$ to $u = s^{-1}(s)$ and applying the Inverse Function Derivative Rule yields:

$$\frac{du}{dt} = \frac{du}{ds}\cdot\frac{ds}{dt} = \frac{1}{\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|}\cdot\frac{ds}{dt} \implies \left\|\frac{d\mathbf{P}}{dt}\right\| = \|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|\cdot\frac{1}{\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|}\cdot\frac{ds}{dt} = \frac{ds}{dt}$$

💡 几何结论：反函数的导数 $\frac{1}{\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|}$ 把曲线内禀拉伸率 $\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|$ 彻底约分抵消了！现在相机的真实物理速率 $100\%$ 听命于你设计的缓动函数 $\frac{ds}{dt}$！ 💡 Geometric Takeaway: The inverse derivative $\frac{1}{\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|}$ cancels $\|\mathbf{P}'(u)\|$ exactly! Physical camera speed now obeys your easing curve $\frac{ds}{dt}$ $100\%$!

Hermite 缓入缓出 ($3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3$) Hermite Smoothstep ($3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3$)

在物理世界中，沉重的电影摄影机具有惯性，起止瞬间的速度必须为 $0$。令归一化时间 $\tau = t/T \in [0,1]$，满足 $e(0)=0, e(1)=1$ 且导数 $e'(0)=e'(1)=0$ 的唯一三次多项式正是 Smoothstep： Physical camera rigs have inertia and must start/stop with zero velocity. The unique cubic on $\tau \in [0,1]$ with $e(0)=0, e(1)=1$ and $e'(0)=e'(1)=0$ is Smoothstep:

$$e(\tau) = 3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3, \qquad e'(\tau) = 6\tau(1-\tau)$$
Frenet Inflection Trap

Frenet 标架在拐点为何瞬间翻转 $180^\circ$？ Why Frenet Frames Flip $180^\circ$ at Inflections

微分几何中的 Frenet 标架用切向量导数定义主法线 $\mathbf{N}$： The Frenet–Serret frame defines the principal normal $\mathbf{N}$ from curvature:

$$\mathbf{T} = \frac{\mathbf{P}'}{\|\mathbf{P}'\|}, \quad \mathbf{N} = \frac{\mathbf{T}'}{\|\mathbf{T}'\|}, \quad \mathbf{B} = \mathbf{T}\times\mathbf{N}$$

⚠️ 当 S 型轨道从左转弯过渡到右转弯时，中间必经过曲率 $\mathbf{T}' = \mathbf{0}$ 的拐点！跨越拐点前后，$\mathbf{T}'$ 的方向从指向左侧圆心突变为指向右侧圆心，导致 $\mathbf{N}$ 和 $\mathbf{B}$ 瞬间翻转 $180^\circ$！ ⚠️ Crossing an S-curve inflection ($\mathbf{T}'=\mathbf{0}$), the center of curvature jumps from left to right, flipping $\mathbf{N}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ by $180^\circ$ in a single frame!

World-Up 正交化与过弯倾侧 (Banking) World-Up Stabilization & Smooth Banking

工业界运镜弃用裸 Frenet 标架，改用恒定的世界天顶 $\mathbf{U}_w = (0,1,0)^T$ 与视线前向 $\mathbf{T}$ 叉乘构建正交标架，并可选地将有符号曲率 $\kappa(s)$ 作为平滑滚转角 $\phi_{\text{bank}} = k\cdot\kappa(s)$ 绕 $\mathbf{T}$ 旋转： Production cameras replace raw Frenet normals with a stable world-up cross product, plus optional roll angle $\phi_{\text{bank}} = k\cdot\kappa(s)$ proportional to signed curvature:

$$\mathbf{R} = \frac{\mathbf{T}\times\mathbf{U}_w}{\|\mathbf{T}\times\mathbf{U}_w\|}, \qquad \mathbf{U}_{\text{cam}} = \mathbf{R}\times\mathbf{T}$$
