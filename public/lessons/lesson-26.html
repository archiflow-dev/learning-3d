角色动画与骨骼蒙皮：Bind Pose 逆矩阵、线性混合蒙皮 (LBS) 与双四元数蒙皮 (DQS) Skeletal Animation & Skinning: Inverse Bind Pose, Linear Blend Skinning (LBS) & Dual Quaternions (DQS)

3Blue1Brown 坐标系切换与矩阵流形直觉 • 为什么两个旋转矩阵求平均会变成奇异降维矩阵？ 3Blue1Brown Change-of-Basis & Manifold Intuition • Why Averaging Two Rotations Collapses Volume

从绑定姿态逆矩阵 $M_i = T_i B_i^{-1}$ 到糖果纸坍缩 ($\det=0$) 与双四元数蒙皮 (DQS) From Inverse Bind Matrices $M_i = T_i B_i^{-1}$ to Candy-Wrapper Collapse ($\det=0$) & Dual Quaternions

在现代 3D 游戏与电影工业中，角色网格有数万个顶点，但美术师只需要摆动几十根虚拟骨头（Skeleton Rig），皮肤就会自然地跟随关节弯曲。这背后的核心公式是线性混合蒙皮 (Linear Blend Skinning, LBS)：$\mathbf{v}' = \sum_{i} w_i T_i B_i^{-1} \mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}}$。 要真正吃透骨骼动画，必须想通两个深刻的几何问题： 第一，为什么每根骨头的当前矩阵 $T_i$ 右边必须先乘一个绑定姿态逆矩阵 $B_i^{-1}$？如果漏掉它会发生什么？ 第二，为什么当角色前臂扭转 $180^\circ$ 时，LBS 会把整条手臂在中间拧成一个半径为 $0$ 的死结（Candy-Wrapper 糖果纸坍缩伪影），而双四元数蒙皮 (Dual Quaternion Skinning, DQS) 又是如何沿着刚体运动流形 $\text{SE}(3)$ 的测地弧完美保住体积的？ In modern 3D games and films, artists animate tens of thousands of skin vertices by moving a few dozen bones via Linear Blend Skinning (LBS): $\mathbf{v}' = \sum_i w_i T_i B_i^{-1} \mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}}$. Two geometric questions lie at the heart of character skinning: First, why must every bone's animated matrix $T_i$ be right-multiplied by its Inverse Bind Pose Matrix $B_i^{-1}$? Second, why does twisting a forearm by $180^\circ$ under LBS pinch the wrist cross-section to a zero-radius point (the notorious Candy-Wrapper Collapse), and how does Dual Quaternion Skinning (DQS) preserve $100\%$ volume along the $\text{SE}(3)$ manifold?

MIT 6.837 L06：从世界空间退回骨骼局部空间 ($B_i^{-1}$)，再随骨骼前往新世界姿态 ($T_i$) MIT 6.837 L06: Un-transforming from Model Space to Bone-Local Space ($B_i^{-1}$), then posing into World Space ($T_i$)

步骤 A：单根骨头的“先退回局部、再推向新世界”两步曲 Step A: Two-Step Basis Change for a Single Bone

建模师导出的初始角色网格处于 T-Pose（绑定姿态 Bind Pose），顶点 $\mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}}$ 存储在模型全局坐标系中。 设第 $i$ 根骨头在绑定姿态下的局部到全局变换矩阵为 $B_i = B_{\text{parent}} L_{i,\text{bind}}$。 要想知道顶点 $\mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}}$ 绑在第 $i$ 根骨头上时的局部坐标 $\mathbf{v}_{\text{local}, i}$，我们必须先用逆矩阵 $B_i^{-1}$ 把它从全局空间拉回第 $i$ 根骨头的局部原点： The mesh is authored in a rest Bind Pose (T-Pose) with vertices $\mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}}$ in Model Space. Let bone $i$'s local-to-model matrix in the bind pose be $B_i = B_{\text{parent}} L_{i,\text{bind}}$. To find the vertex's constant coordinates $\mathbf{v}_{\text{local},i}$ in bone $i$'s local frame, we first apply $B_i^{-1}$:

$$\mathbf{v}_{\text{local}, i} = B_i^{-1} \mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}} \quad \xrightarrow{\text{播放到当前动画帧 } T_i(t)} \quad \mathbf{v}_i'(t) = \underbrace{T_i(t)\, B_i^{-1}}_{M_i(t)} \mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}}$$

验证特例：当角色还停留在初始 T-Pose 时，$T_i(0) = B_i$，此时 $M_i(0) = B_i B_i^{-1} = I$（单位矩阵），所有顶点纹丝不动！ Sanity check: In the rest pose, $T_i(0) = B_i$, so $M_i(0) = B_i B_i^{-1} = I$ (identity) and the mesh stays untouched!

步骤 B：多骨骼权重分区统一 (Partition of Unity) 与线性混合 Step B: Partition of Unity & Linear Blend Skinning (LBS)

对于位于关节折痕处的顶点，我们为其分配一组非负权重 $\{w_i\}$，满足单位分解条件 (Partition of Unity) $\sum_{i=1}^k w_i = 1$（保证整体刚体平移时的仿射不变性）。利用矩阵乘法分配律，可将顶点位置加权平均化简为先混合矩阵 $M_i$： For vertices near a joint crease, we assign non-negative weights $\{w_i\}$ satisfying Partition of Unity $\sum_{i=1}^k w_i = 1$ (preserving affine invariance under rigid translations):

$$\mathbf{v}'(t) = \sum_{i=1}^k w_i \Big(T_i(t)\, B_i^{-1}\, \mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}}\Big) = \left(\sum_{i=1}^k w_i M_i(t)\right) \mathbf{v}_{\text{bind}}$$

💡 在现代顶点着色器 (Vertex Shader) 中，CPU 只需每帧上传骨骼矩阵数组 $M_i = T_i B_i^{-1}$，每个顶点读取 `uvec4 boneIds` 与 `vec4 weights` 执行 4 次矩阵乘加即可！ 💡 In a GPU Vertex Shader, the CPU uploads the bone palette $M_i = T_i B_i^{-1}$ once per frame, and each vertex blends 4 matrices via `uvec4 boneIds` and `vec4 weights`!

LBS Matrix Defect • Candy-Wrapper Collapse

当骨头扭转 $180^\circ$ 时，中点混合矩阵行列式直接归零 ($\det = 0$)！ At $180^\circ$ Twist, the Midpoint Matrix Has $\det = 0$!

设上臂保持单位矩阵 $R_1 = I$，前臂绕长轴 $x$ 扭转角 $\theta$。在手腕中点（权重 $w_1 = w_2 = 0.5$）处，LBS 对两个旋转矩阵求算术平均： Let the upper arm be $R_1 = I$ and the forearm twist around the $x$-axis by angle $\theta$. At the midpoint ($w_1=w_2=0.5$), LBS averages the two matrices:

$$M_{0.5}(\theta) = \frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} + \frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ 0 & \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix} \xrightarrow{\theta = 180^\circ} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{0} \\ 0 & \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{0} \end{bmatrix}$$

任意角度 $\theta$ 下，$M_{0.5}(\theta)$ 对圆柱横截面半径的缩放因子为 $\sqrt{\left(\frac{1+\cos\theta}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sin\theta}{2}\right)^2} = \cos\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)$。当 $\theta=180^\circ$ 时，$\cos(90^\circ)=0$，整圈手腕完全坍缩成一个点！ For any angle $\theta$, the cross-section radius scales by $\sqrt{\left(\frac{1+\cos\theta}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sin\theta}{2}\right)^2} = \cos(\theta/2)$, collapsing to $0$ at $\theta=180^\circ$!

Kavan et al. 2007 • Dual Quaternion Skinning (DQS)

单位双四元数 $\hat{q} = q_r + \epsilon q_d$ 流形投影：半径恒为 $100\%$ Unit Dual Quaternion $\hat{q} = q_r + \epsilon q_d$: $100\%$ Volume Retention

引入幂零对偶单位 $\epsilon$（满足 $\epsilon^2 = 0$），将每根骨头的旋转四元数 $q_r$ 与平移向量 $\mathbf{t} = (t_x, t_y, t_z)$ 打包为单位双四元数 $\hat{q} = q_r + \epsilon\frac{1}{2}(0, \mathbf{t})q_r$。混合时先统一四元数符号（$\text{dot}(q_{r,0}, q_{r,i}) \ge 0$），再加权求和并除以实部模长归一化： Using the dual unit $\epsilon$ ($\epsilon^2 = 0$), encode rotation $q_r$ and translation $\mathbf{t}$ as $\hat{q} = q_r + \epsilon\frac{1}{2}(0,\mathbf{t})q_r$. Align hemisphere signs ($\text{dot}(q_{r,0}, q_{r,i}) \ge 0$), blend linearly, and normalize:

$$\hat{q}_{\text{blend}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^k w_i \hat{q}_i}{\left\|\sum_{i=1}^k w_i q_{r,i}\right\|} \implies r_{\text{DQS}}(\theta) \equiv 1 \quad (\forall \theta \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ))$$
