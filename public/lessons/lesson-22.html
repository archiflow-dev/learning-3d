屏幕空间仿射插值的破产与 $1/Z$ 双曲透视校正推导 Failure of Screen-Space Affine Interpolation & Step-by-Step $1/Z$ Hyperbolic Derivation

MIT 6.837 L14 & UC Davis ECS 175 L10：为什么屏幕上均匀移动 $s$，对应三维世界是非均匀移动 $t$？ MIT 6.837 L14 & UC Davis ECS 175 L10: Why uniform screen steps $s$ map to non-uniform 3D steps $t$

步骤 A：透视除法如何扭曲比例参数 $t \mapsto s$ Step A: How Perspective Division Warps $t \mapsto s$

考虑三维相机空间中的一条线段 $\mathbf{P}(t) = (1-t)\mathbf{P}_0 + t\mathbf{P}_1$，其中 $t \in [0, 1]$ 是三维世界中的真实均匀比例。两端点的正深度记为 $Z_0, Z_1 > 0$，对应的屏幕投影坐标为 $x_0 = \frac{X_0}{Z_0}$ 和 $x_1 = \frac{X_1}{Z_1}$。 线段上点 $\mathbf{P}(t)$ 投射到屏幕上的位置 $x(t)$ 为： Consider a 3D line segment $\mathbf{P}(t) = (1-t)\mathbf{P}_0 + t\mathbf{P}_1$ where $t \in [0,1]$ is the true 3D linear parameter. Let the positive depths be $Z_0, Z_1 > 0$ and screen coordinates be $x_0 = X_0/Z_0$, $x_1 = X_1/Z_1$. Projecting $\mathbf{P}(t)$ onto the screen gives:

$$x(t) = \frac{(1-t)X_0 + t X_1}{(1-t)Z_0 + t Z_1} = \frac{(1-t)Z_0 x_0 + t Z_1 x_1}{(1-t)Z_0 + t Z_1}$$

而在屏幕上，光栅化器是以屏幕像素比例 $s \in [0, 1]$ 均匀步进的：$x(s) = (1-s)x_0 + s x_1$。对比两式中 $x_0$ 与 $x_1$ 的组合系数，立刻得到屏幕比例 $s$ 与三维比例 $t$ 的精确双曲变换关系： On screen, the rasterizer steps uniformly in screen fraction $s \in [0,1]$: $x(s) = (1-s)x_0 + s x_1$. Matching coefficients of $x_0$ and $x_1$ reveals the exact hyperbolic relation between $s$ and $t$:

$$s = \frac{t Z_1}{(1-t)Z_0 + t Z_1} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad t = \frac{s / Z_0}{(1-s)/Z_1 + s / Z_0}$$
步骤 B：奇迹出现 —— $1/Z$ 与 $u/Z$ 在屏幕空间严格线性！ Step B: The Miracle — $1/Z$ and $u/Z$ Are Strictly Linear in Screen Space!

将 $t = \frac{s/Z_0}{(1-s)/Z_1 + s/Z_0}$ 代回三维空间线性变化的深度 $Z(t) = (1-t)Z_0 + t Z_1$ 和纹理坐标 $u(t) = (1-t)u_0 + t u_1$： Substitute $t = \frac{s/Z_0}{(1-s)/Z_1 + s/Z_0}$ back into the 3D-linear depth $Z(t) = (1-t)Z_0 + t Z_1$ and texture coordinate $u(t) = (1-t)u_0 + t u_1$:

$$\frac{1}{Z(s)} = (1-s)\frac{1}{Z_0} + s\frac{1}{Z_1}, \qquad \frac{u(s)}{Z(s)} = (1-s)\frac{u_0}{Z_0} + s\frac{u_1}{Z_1}$$

推广到三角形的屏幕空间重心坐标 $(\alpha_s, \beta_s, \gamma_s)$，GPU 硬件只需对倒数 $\frac{1}{Z}$ 和除以深度后的属性 $\frac{u}{Z}, \frac{v}{Z}$ 做屏幕线性插值，最后在每个像素处做一次除法即可恢复完全无扭曲的真实纹理坐标： Generalizing to screen-space barycentric coordinates $(\alpha_s, \beta_s, \gamma_s)$, GPU hardware linearly interpolates $\frac{1}{Z}$ and $\frac{u}{Z}, \frac{v}{Z}$ across screen pixels, then performs one division per pixel:

$$u_{\text{correct}}(x,y) = \frac{\alpha_s \frac{u_0}{Z_0} + \beta_s \frac{u_1}{Z_1} + \gamma_s \frac{u_2}{Z_2}}{\alpha_s \frac{1}{Z_0} + \beta_s \frac{1}{Z_1} + \gamma_s \frac{1}{Z_2}}$$

Magnification $\rho < 1$ texel/px

双线性过滤 (Bilinear Filtering) Bilinear Filtering ($2\times 2$ Texels)

当相机凑近纹理表面（1 个纹素放大到覆盖几十个像素）时，最近邻采样会暴露锯齿马赛克。双线性过滤读取周围 $2\times 2$ 个纹素中心，按小数偏移 $(s_f, t_f) \in [0,1]^2$ 做两次水平 `lerp` 与一次垂直 `lerp`： When zooming in close ($\rho < 1$), nearest-neighbor reveals blocky pixels. Bilinear filtering samples the $2\times 2$ surrounding texel centers and blends them smoothly via two horizontal and one vertical `lerp`:

$$\mathbf{C} = \text{lerp}\big(\text{lerp}(\mathbf{C}_{00},\mathbf{C}_{10},s_f),\;\text{lerp}(\mathbf{C}_{01},\mathbf{C}_{11},s_f),\;t_f\big)$$
Minification & Jacobian $\rho \gg 1$ texels/px

雅可比导数与 Mipmap 层级 $d$ Jacobian Footprint & Mipmap Level $d$

设纹素坐标 $(U, V) = (uW, vH)$。当屏幕步进 $(\Delta x=1, \Delta y=1)$ 时，纹素空间的两个偏导切向量 $\mathbf{g}_x = (\frac{\partial U}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial V}{\partial x})^T$ 与 $\mathbf{g}_y = (\frac{\partial U}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial V}{\partial y})^T$ 张成了像素的平行四边形脚印： Let $(U,V) = (uW, vH)$. Moving 1 pixel in $x$ and $y$ spans two tangent vectors $\mathbf{g}_x = (\frac{\partial U}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial V}{\partial x})^T$ and $\mathbf{g}_y = (\frac{\partial U}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial V}{\partial y})^T$ forming the pixel's footprint in texel space:

$$\rho = \max\big(\|\mathbf{g}_x\|, \|\mathbf{g}_y\|\big), \qquad d = \log_2(\rho)$$

💡 1/3 显存定律：每升一级长宽减半（面积变为 $1/4$），总显存开销 $\sum_{k=0}^\infty \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^k = \frac{1}{1-1/4} = \frac{4}{3}$，仅比原图多出 33.3%！ 💡 1/3 Memory Rule: Each level quarters the area, so total memory is $\sum_{k=0}^\infty (1/4)^k = 4/3$, only 33.3% extra!

Grazing Angles $\|\mathbf{g}_y\| \gg \|\mathbf{g}_x\|$

三线性与各向异性过滤 (AF) Trilinear & Anisotropic Filtering (AF)

三线性过滤在 $\lfloor d \rfloor$ 与 $\lceil d \rceil$ 两层 Mipmap 之间再做一次线性插值（共 8 个纹素采样），消除层级跳变带。而在掠射角观察地面时，$\|\mathbf{g}_y\| \gg \|\mathbf{g}_x\|$，像素脚印是瘦长椭圆！标准 Mipmap 迁就长轴 $\|\mathbf{g}_y\|$ 会把横向也糊掉；各向异性过滤 ($N\times\text{AF}$) 改在匹配短轴 $\|\mathbf{g}_x\|$ 的清晰层级上，沿长轴方向连续采样 $N$ 次！ Trilinear filtering blends between levels $\lfloor d \rfloor$ and $\lceil d \rceil$ (8 texels). At grazing angles ($\|\mathbf{g}_y\| \gg \|\mathbf{g}_x\|$), the footprint is a long narrow ellipse. Standard Mipmaps blur horizontally by picking $\max$; Anisotropic Filtering ($N\times\text{AF}$) picks the sharper level matching $\min(\|\mathbf{g}_x\|,\|\mathbf{g}_y\|)$ and marches $N$ samples along the major axis!

为什么法线贴图是蓝紫色的？如何从三角形边向量解出 $[\mathbf{T} \mid \mathbf{B} \mid \mathbf{N}]$？ Why Normal Maps Are Bluish-Purple & Solving $[\mathbf{T} \mid \mathbf{B} \mid \mathbf{N}]$ from Triangle Edges

如果直接在法线贴图里存储世界空间法线，一旦模型旋转或骨骼弯曲，贴图就完全失效了！因此我们以每个顶点自身的局部表面为基准建立正交坐标系：沿着纹理横轴 $+u$ 的方向称为切线 (Tangent, $\hat{\mathbf{T}}$)，沿着纹理纵轴 $+v$ 的方向称为副切线 (Bitangent, $\hat{\mathbf{B}}$)，垂直表面向外为宏观法线 ($\hat{\mathbf{N}}$)。 平坦表面的局部法线为 $(0, 0, 1)^T$，经 $\text{RGB} = 0.5\mathbf{n} + 0.5$ 映射到 $[0,1]^3$ 后就是 $(0.5, 0.5, 1.0)$（蓝紫色）。给定三角形两条边 $\Delta\mathbf{P}_1 = \mathbf{P}_1 - \mathbf{P}_0, \Delta\mathbf{P}_2 = \mathbf{P}_2 - \mathbf{P}_0$ 及其 UV 差值 $(\Delta u_i, \Delta v_i)$： Storing world-space normals in a texture breaks whenever the mesh rotates or deforms. Instead, we define a local orthonormal frame on the surface: Tangent ($\hat{\mathbf{T}}$) along $+u$, Bitangent ($\hat{\mathbf{B}}$) along $+v$, and Normal ($\hat{\mathbf{N}}$) outward. An unperturbed normal $(0,0,1)^T$ encodes via $0.5\mathbf{n}+0.5$ to $(0.5, 0.5, 1.0)$ (bluish-purple). From triangle edges $\Delta\mathbf{P}_1, \Delta\mathbf{P}_2$ and UV deltas $(\Delta u_i, \Delta v_i)$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{T} \\ \mathbf{B} \end{bmatrix} = \frac{1}{\Delta u_1 \Delta v_2 - \Delta v_1 \Delta u_2} \begin{bmatrix} \Delta v_2 & -\Delta v_1 \\ -\Delta u_2 & \Delta u_1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \Delta\mathbf{P}_1 \\ \Delta\mathbf{P}_2 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \mathbf{n}_{\text{world}} = \text{normalize}\Big(\underbrace{\big[\hat{\mathbf{T}} \;\; \hat{\mathbf{B}} \;\; \hat{\mathbf{N}}\big]}_{\text{TBN Matrix}} \, (2\mathbf{C}_{\text{tex}} - \mathbf{1})\Big)$$

🔍 观察指南：为什么对角线会折断？ 🔍 Why Does the Diagonal Kink?

一个矩形由两个三角形 $(\mathbf{V}_0, \mathbf{V}_1, \mathbf{V}_2)$ 和 $(\mathbf{V}_0, \mathbf{V}_2, \mathbf{V}_3)$ 拼成。每个三角形在屏幕内部各自按平面重心坐标线性插值 $(u,v)$，但两个三角形的三个顶点深度组合不同，导致它们的错误仿射平面在公共对角线 $\mathbf{V}_0\mathbf{V}_2$ 处斜率突变！切换到 $1/Z$ 透视校正 后，折断瞬间消失。 A quad is split into two triangles $(\mathbf{V}_0,\mathbf{V}_1,\mathbf{V}_2)$ and $(\mathbf{V}_0,\mathbf{V}_2,\mathbf{V}_3)$. Affine interpolation fits a different screen-linear plane to each triangle's 3 vertices, creating a sharp slope discontinuity across the shared diagonal $\mathbf{V}_0\mathbf{V}_2$. Switching to $1/Z$ Perspective-Correct eliminates the kink completely!
