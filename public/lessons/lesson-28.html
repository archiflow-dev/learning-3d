第一部分 · 势能梯度与轴向阻尼投影 Part 1 · Potential Gradient & Axial Dashpot Projection

1. 为什么阻尼力必须投影到弹簧轴线上？ 1. Why Must Dashpot Damping Project onto the Spring Axis?

连接质点 $\mathbf{x}_i$ 与 $\mathbf{x}_j$、原长为 $L_0$ 的弹簧，其弹性势能为 $E(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{x}_j) = \frac{1}{2}k_s\big(\|\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i\| - L_0\big)^2$。对 $\mathbf{x}_i$ 求负梯度（令 $\mathbf{d} = \mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i,\;\hat{\mathbf{d}} = \mathbf{d}/\|\mathbf{d}\|$），立即得到三维向量胡克定律：

For a spring of rest length $L_0$ connecting $\mathbf{x}_i$ and $\mathbf{x}_j$, its elastic potential energy is $E(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{x}_j) = \frac{1}{2}k_s(\|\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i\| - L_0)^2$. Taking the negative gradient with respect to $\mathbf{x}_i$ (with $\mathbf{d}=\mathbf{x}_j-\mathbf{x}_i,\;\hat{\mathbf{d}}=\mathbf{d}/\|\mathbf{d}\|$) yields 3D Vector Hooke's Law:

$$\mathbf{F}_{i\leftarrow j}^{\text{spring}} = -\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_i} E = k_s\big(\|\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i\| - L_0\big)\,\hat{\mathbf{d}}, \qquad \mathbf{F}_{j\leftarrow i}^{\text{spring}} = -\mathbf{F}_{i\leftarrow j}^{\text{spring}}$$

3Blue1Brown 几何追问：如果为了消耗多余振动直接写全局阻尼 $\mathbf{F}^{\text{viscous}} = -k_d \mathbf{v}_i$，会发生什么灾难？当整块布料在空中自由下落或刚体旋转时，弹簧根本没有伸长，但 $-k_d\mathbf{v}_i$ 却像把布料泡在浓稠糖浆里一样拖慢整体运动！真正物理正确的弹簧轴向阻尼器 (Axial Dashpot) 只阻尼弹簧长度的变化率 $\dot{L} = \frac{d}{dt}\|\mathbf{x}_j - \mathbf{x}_i\| = (\mathbf{v}_j - \mathbf{v}_i)\cdot\hat{\mathbf{d}}$：

3Blue1Brown Geometric Insight: If you apply naive global damping $\mathbf{F} = -k_d \mathbf{v}_i$, rigid-body falling or rotation is falsely damped as if submerged in molasses! True internal Axial Dashpot Damping only opposes the rate of change of spring length $\dot{L} = (\mathbf{v}_j - \mathbf{v}_i)\cdot\hat{\mathbf{d}}$:

$$\mathbf{F}_{i\leftarrow j}^{\text{damp}} = k_d\Big((\mathbf{v}_j - \mathbf{v}_i)\cdot\hat{\mathbf{d}}\Big)\hat{\mathbf{d}}$$

💡 当相对速度 $\Delta\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_j - \mathbf{v}_i$ 垂直于弹簧轴 $\hat{\mathbf{d}}$（即纯刚体旋转）时，$(\Delta\mathbf{v}\cdot\hat{\mathbf{d}}) = 0$，轴向阻尼力精确为零，绝不干扰刚体摆动！

💡 When relative velocity $\Delta\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_j - \mathbf{v}_i$ is perpendicular to $\hat{\mathbf{d}}$ (pure rigid rotation), $(\Delta\mathbf{v}\cdot\hat{\mathbf{d}}) = 0$, so axial damping is identically zero!

第二部分 · 布料三大弹簧拓扑与零空间 (Nullspace) Part 2 · Cloth Spring Topologies & Deformation Nullspace

2. 为什么仅有横竖弹簧的网格会像手风琴一样坍缩？ 2. Why Does a Grid with Only Structural Springs Collapse?

把一块布料离散为 $N\times N$ 质点网格（Provot 1995 模型），为什么必须同时布置三种拓扑弹簧？从“零能量变形模态（刚度矩阵零空间 Nullspace）”一看便知：

In Provot's (1995) $N\times N$ mass-spring cloth model, why do we need three distinct spring topologies? Look at the zero-energy deformation modes (nullspace):

  • ① 结构弹簧 (Structural: $(i,j)\leftrightarrow(i\pm 1,j),(i,j\pm 1)$, $L_0=h$)： 抵抗水平经线与垂直纬线拉伸（Stretch）。但正方形压扁成菱形（平行四边形错切）时四条边长完全不变！因此仅有结构弹簧时，剪切变形位于零空间中，布料一斜拉就会无阻力坍缩成一条线。 Resists horizontal/vertical stretching. However, shearing a square into a rhombus keeps all 4 edge lengths unchanged! Without diagonals, in-plane shear lies in the zero-energy nullspace.
  • ② 剪切弹簧 (Shear: $(i,j)\leftrightarrow(i\pm 1,j\pm 1)$, $L_0=\sqrt{2}h$)： 连接每个方格的两条对角线。一旦正方形发生菱形错切，一条对角线必然拉长、另一条必然缩短，从而锁死平面内错切自由度！但沿着网格线把布料像折纸一样 $180^\circ$ 对折时，结构边和对角线长度全都不变！ Connects quad diagonals ($L_0=\sqrt{2}h$), locking in-plane rhombus shear. Yet folding the mesh $180^\circ$ like origami along a grid line leaves both structural and diagonal lengths unchanged!
  • ③ 弯曲弹簧 (Bending / Flexion: $(i,j)\leftrightarrow(i\pm 2,j),(i,j\pm 2)$, $L_0=2h$)： 每隔一个顶点跨步连接！一旦相邻两个面片向法向折弯，跨步端点的欧氏距离立刻小于 $2h$，弯曲弹簧便产生回弹力，赋予丝绸、棉布或厚牛仔布不同的悬垂褶皱挺括度。 Connects every second vertex ($L_0=2h$). Out-of-plane folding immediately shortens the 2-hop distance below $2h$, resisting sharp creases and controlling cloth drape stiffness.
第三部分 · 刚度危机与位置约束 (PBD / Provot) Part 3 · The Stiffness Crisis & Constraint Projection

3. 刚度危机 $\Delta t_{\text{crit}} = 2\sqrt{m/k_s}$ 与碰撞位置修正 3. Stiffness Crisis $\Delta t_{\text{crit}} = 2\sqrt{m/k_s}$ & Collision Projection

真实的棉布和牛仔布几乎不可拉伸（需要极大的刚度系数 $k_s$）。但在上一课（L27）我们证明了半隐式欧拉的稳定充要条件是 $h\,\omega_{\max} < 2$。由于弹簧固有频率 $\omega = \sqrt{k_s/m}$，最大允许时间步长为：

Real fabrics barely stretch (requiring huge stiffness $k_s$). Recall from Lesson 27 that Symplectic Euler requires $h\,\omega_{\max} < 2$. Since natural frequency $\omega = \sqrt{k_s/m}$, the critical time step is:

$$\Delta t_{\text{crit}} = \frac{2}{\omega_{\max}} \propto 2\sqrt{\frac{m}{k_s}}, \qquad \Delta\mathbf{x}_i = +\frac{w_i}{w_i+w_j}\big(\|\mathbf{d}\| - L_0\big)\hat{\mathbf{d}}$$

当 $k_s$ 增大 100 倍，$\Delta t_{\text{crit}}$ 就被迫缩小 10 倍，否则单步过冲（Overshoot）会让布料瞬间炸裂！现代实时布料引擎（Provot 1995 / Müller PBD 2007）采用温和刚度力积分 $+$ Gauss-Seidel 距离约束直接投影，并对球体碰撞执行符号距离场（SDF）表面投影 $\mathbf{x}_i \leftarrow \mathbf{c} + (R+\epsilon)\frac{\mathbf{x}_i-\mathbf{c}}{\|\mathbf{x}_i-\mathbf{c}\|}$ 并消去法向穿入速度。

Increasing $k_s$ by $100\times$ shrinks $\Delta t_{\text{crit}}$ by $10\times$! Real-time engines (Provot 1995 / Position-Based Dynamics) combine moderate spring integration with iterative Gauss–Seidel distance constraint projection and sphere SDF collision projection.

1. 连续体 vs 离散质点网络 1. Continuum vs. Discrete Mass-Spring Network

虽然有限元方法（FEM）直接从连续弹性力学偏微分方程（PDE）出发，但质点弹簧网络（Mass-Spring Network）将表面离散为质点 $m_i$ 与无质量弹簧边 $(i,j)$，把偏微分方程直接转化为第 27 课学过的常微分方程组 $\dot{\mathbf{Y}} = \mathbf{f}(\mathbf{Y})$，具备极高的工程实现性价比。

While Finite Element Methods (FEM) discretize continuum PDEs directly, Mass-Spring networks replace a surface with point masses $m_i$ and massless springs $(i,j)$, reducing cloth PDEs immediately to the coupled ODE system $\dot{\mathbf{Y}} = \mathbf{f}(\mathbf{Y})$ from Lesson 27.

2. 内力与外力解耦管线 2. Internal vs. External Force Accumulation

每一帧步进前，先把每个粒子的力累加器清零 $\mathbf{F}_i \leftarrow \mathbf{0}$；随后叠加外力（重力 $m_i\mathbf{g}$、气动风阻 $\frac{1}{2}\rho C_d A ((\mathbf{v}_{\text{wind}}-\mathbf{v})\cdot\mathbf{n})\mathbf{n}$），再遍历全部弹簧边计算胡克弹力与轴向阻尼力，并按牛顿第三定律成对施加 $\mathbf{F}_i \mathrel{+}= \mathbf{F}_{i\leftarrow j},\;\mathbf{F}_j \mathrel{-}= \mathbf{F}_{i\leftarrow j}$。

Each frame clears force accumulators $\mathbf{F}_i \leftarrow \mathbf{0}$, adds external forces (gravity $m_i\mathbf{g}$, aerodynamic wind normal pressure), and loops over every spring edge once, applying equal and opposite forces $\mathbf{F}_i \mathrel{+}= \mathbf{F}_{i\leftarrow j}$ and $\mathbf{F}_j \mathrel{-}= \mathbf{F}_{i\leftarrow j}$ by Newton's 3rd Law.

3. 固定针脚 (Pinned Constraints) 与碰撞 3. Pinned Vertices & Collision Handling

对于被挂钩固定的顶点（如窗帘顶边或旗杆角点），只需将其逆质量设为 $w_i = 1/m_i = 0$（或每步强制清零其速度与位移增量 $\Delta\mathbf{x}_i = \mathbf{0}$）。对于隐式曲面碰撞（如球体 $\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{c}\| - R < 0$），将穿入点沿法向推回球面并抹除向内法向速度分量。

Pinned vertices simply take inverse mass $w_i = 1/m_i = 0$ (zero velocity and position delta). Implicit surface collisions ($f(\mathbf{x}) < 0$) project penetrating vertices back onto $f(\mathbf{x}) = 0$ along $\nabla f$ and cancel inward normal velocity.
