3Blue1Brown 几何与拓扑直觉 • 为什么外积矩阵能拍扁三维物体，而环绕数计数能判定任意三维阴影？ 3Blue1Brown Geometric & Topological Intuition • Outer-Product Projection & Winding-Number Shadow Volumes

从齐次外积投影矩阵 $M = (\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{L})I - \mathbf{L}\mathbf{\Pi}^T$ 到卡马克反转 (Carmack's Reverse) From Homogeneous Outer-Product Projection $M = (\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{L})I - \mathbf{L}\mathbf{\Pi}^T$ to Carmack's Reverse

在实时光栅化管线中，每个三角形都是独立送入 GPU 的——一个像素在着色时根本不知道场景里还有哪些其他三角形挡在了它和光源之间！为了在光栅化中生成精确的几何硬阴影，图形学先驱们发明了两套极具数学美感的几何武器： 第一，Jim Blinn (1988) 发现将三维顶点沿点光源射线投射到任意平面 $\mathbf{\Pi}$ 上，竟然等价于乘以一个由内积减外积构成的 $4\times 4$ 齐次矩阵 $M_{\text{shadow}} = (\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{L})I - \mathbf{L}\mathbf{\Pi}^T$； 第二，Frank Crow (1977) 与 John Carmack (2000, Doom 3) 利用拓扑学中的约旦曲线环绕数定理 (Winding Number Theorem)，将遮挡物的轮廓边挤出为半无穷阴影体积锥，仅凭硬件模板缓冲 (Stencil Buffer) 的 `+1 / -1` 整数加减法，就实现了像素级绝对锋利的全场景自阴影！ In rasterization, each triangle is processed in isolation—a pixel shader has no idea which other triangles block its line of sight to the light! Pioneers invented two mathematically elegant geometric solutions: First, Jim Blinn (1988) showed that projecting 3D vertices from a light $\mathbf{L}$ onto a plane $\mathbf{\Pi}$ is a single $4\times 4$ matrix built from an inner product minus an outer product: $M_{\text{shadow}} = (\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{L})I - \mathbf{L}\mathbf{\Pi}^T$. Second, Frank Crow (1977) and John Carmack (2000, Doom 3) applied the topological Winding Number Theorem by extruding silhouette edges into semi-infinite shadow cones and counting `+1 / -1` wall crossings in the Stencil Buffer!

UC Davis ECS 175 L18：为什么射线-平面求交的分母可以被齐次坐标的 $w$ 分量完美吸收？ UC Davis ECS 175 L18: How homogeneous division by $w$ absorbs the nonlinear ray-plane denominator

步骤 A：光源射线 $\mathbf{L} \to \mathbf{V}$ 与平面 $\mathbf{\Pi}$ 求交 Step A: Ray $\mathbf{L} \to \mathbf{V}$ Intersecting Plane $\mathbf{\Pi}$

设地面平面方程为 $n_x x + n_y y + n_z z + d = 0$，写成四维对偶行向量 $\mathbf{\Pi}^T = [n_x, n_y, n_z, d]$。设光源齐次坐标为 $\mathbf{L} = [L_x, L_y, L_z, L_w]^T$（点光源 $L_w=1$，平行光 $L_w=0$），遮挡物顶点为 $\mathbf{V} = [V_x, V_y, V_z, 1]^T$。 连接 $\mathbf{L}$ 与 $\mathbf{V}$ 的直线上任意点为 $\mathbf{P}(t) = \mathbf{L} + t(\mathbf{V} - \mathbf{L})$，代入平面方程 $\mathbf{\Pi}^T \mathbf{P}(t) = 0$： Let the receiver plane be $\mathbf{\Pi}^T = [n_x, n_y, n_z, d]$, light be $\mathbf{L} = [L_x, L_y, L_z, L_w]^T$ ($L_w=1$ for point light, $0$ for directional), and vertex be $\mathbf{V} = [V_x, V_y, V_z, 1]^T$. Substituting $\mathbf{P}(t) = \mathbf{L} + t(\mathbf{V}-\mathbf{L})$ into $\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{P}(t)=0$ gives:

$$t = \frac{\mathbf{\Pi}^T \mathbf{L}}{\mathbf{\Pi}^T(\mathbf{L} - \mathbf{V})} \implies \mathbf{P}_s = \frac{(\mathbf{\Pi}^T \mathbf{L})\mathbf{V} - (\mathbf{\Pi}^T \mathbf{V})\mathbf{L}}{\mathbf{\Pi}^T \mathbf{L} - \mathbf{\Pi}^T \mathbf{V}}$$
步骤 B：齐次外积提取 —— 分子直接化成矩阵乘向量！ Step B: Homogeneous Outer-Product Trick

注意上式的分母 $\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{L} - \mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{V}$ 正好是分子第 4 分量的值！在齐次坐标中，将整个向量乘以非零标量分母不改变三维位置（透视除法除以 $w$ 会自动还原它）。又因为标量乘向量满足 $(\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{V})\mathbf{L} = \mathbf{L}(\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{V}) = (\mathbf{L}\mathbf{\Pi}^T)\mathbf{V}$，我们直接把 $\mathbf{V}$ 提出来： Since homogeneous coordinates are scale-invariant (division by $w$ restores the denominator automatically), and $(\mathbf{\Pi}^T\mathbf{V})\mathbf{L} = (\mathbf{L}\mathbf{\Pi}^T)\mathbf{V}$ by associativity of outer products, we factor $\mathbf{V}$ out to the right:

$$\tilde{\mathbf{P}}_s = \Big(\underbrace{(\mathbf{\Pi}^T \mathbf{L}) I_{4\times 4} - \mathbf{L}\mathbf{\Pi}^T}_{M_{\text{shadow}}}\Big)\mathbf{V}$$

⚠️ 反阴影陷阱 (Anti-Shadow)：若顶点 $\mathbf{V}$ 位于点光源 $\mathbf{L}$ 背离地面的一侧（即 $t < 0$，齐次坐标 $w_s < 0$），直线反向延长也会刺穿地面产生荒谬的“头顶反阴影”，必须在裁剪空间剔除 $w_s \le 0$ 的投影顶点！ ⚠️ Anti-Shadow Trap: If $\mathbf{V}$ lies behind the point light ($t < 0 \iff w_s < 0$), the backward ray still hits the floor! Always clip vertices with $w_s \le 0$.

为什么相机走进阴影锥内部会导致 Z-Pass 瞬间崩溃？卡马克是如何用“从表面往无穷远倒着数”解决这个世纪难题的？ Why Z-Pass breaks when the camera enters a shadow cone, and how John Carmack solved it by counting behind the surface

Step 1 • Silhouette Edges

轮廓边符号相反判据 Opposite-Sign Dot Product Test

阴影锥的侧壁只需要从遮挡物的轮廓边 (Silhouette Edges) 挤出。一条内部共享边 $e=(\mathbf{v}_a,\mathbf{v}_b)$ 是轮廓边，当且仅当它两侧的相邻三角形 $f_1, f_2$ 一个朝向光源、一个背向光源： Shadow volume side quads only need to be extruded from silhouette edges—shared edges where one adjacent triangle faces the light and the other faces away:

$$\big(\mathbf{n}_1 \cdot (\mathbf{L}-\mathbf{c}_1)\big)\cdot\big(\mathbf{n}_2 \cdot (\mathbf{L}-\mathbf{c}_2)\big) < 0$$
Step 2 • Classic Z-Pass

Z-Pass 正向计数 (`+1` 进 / `-1` 出) Z-Pass Counting (`+1` Front / `-1` Back)

先渲染场景写好 Z-Buffer。再关闭颜色和深度写入，渲染阴影体积侧壁：凡是通过深度测试（`Z-Pass`，位于相机与表面点 $\mathbf{P}$ 之间）的前向壁令 `stencil++` ($+1$)，背向壁令 `stencil--` ($-1$)。最终 `stencil > 0` 的像素就在阴影内！ Render scene depths first. Then render shadow volume walls with depth-write off: on Z-Pass (between eye and surface $\mathbf{P}$), front faces increment `stencil++` ($+1$) and back faces decrement `stencil--` ($-1$). Pixels with `stencil > 0` are in shadow!

Step 3 • Carmack's Reverse

Z-Fail 卡马克反转 (防相机穿入崩溃) Carmack's Reverse (`Z-Fail` + Caps)

当相机走进阴影锥内部（或近裁剪面切破前壁）时，`Z-Pass` 漏掉了入口的 `+1`，全场明暗颠倒！卡马克反转 (`Z-Fail`) 改在深度测试失败（位于表面点 $\mathbf{P}$ 后方直到无穷远）时计数：背向壁 `+1`，前向壁 `-1`，并封死朝光顶盖 (Light Cap) 与无穷远底盖 ($w=0$ Dark Cap)，彻底免疫近裁剪面切割！ When the eye is inside a shadow cone, `Z-Pass` misses the entry front wall! Carmack's Reverse (`Z-Fail`) counts walls behind the surface (`Z-Fail`): back faces `+1`, front faces `-1`, with closed Light Cap and $w=0$ Dark Cap!
