3Blue1Brown 相空间与向量场直觉 • 为什么沿着圆的切线走一步，能量就必然按勾股定理爆炸？ 3Blue1Brown Phase-Space Intuition • Why Stepping Along a Circle's Tangent Explodes Energy by Pythagoras

从相空间状态向量 $\mathbf{Y} = [\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{v}]^T$ 到显式欧拉斜边爆炸、辛欧拉保面积 ($\det=1$) 与 RK4 From Phase-Space State $\mathbf{Y} = [\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{v}]^T$ to Explicit Euler Explosion, Symplectic Area Preservation ($\det=1$) & RK4

无论是火花、流体、布料还是天体引力，世间万物的物理运动都由牛顿第二定律 $\mathbf{F} = m\ddot{\mathbf{x}}$ 支配。在计算机里，时间不是连续流淌的，而是以帧步长 $\Delta t = h$ 一格一格跳跃的。 这引发了物理仿真中最著名的三大灵魂追问： 第一，为什么最直观的前向显式欧拉法 (Forward Explicit Euler) 在模拟哪怕最简单的一根无阻尼弹簧时，每走一步能量就会乘以 $(1 + h^2)$，几秒钟内直接炸飞出屏幕？ 第二，为什么仅仅把“先更新位置还是先更新速度”的两行代码顺序调换一下（变成半隐式/辛欧拉 Symplectic Euler），转移矩阵的行列式就奇迹般地恒等于 $\det(A) \equiv 1$，弹簧瞬间变得永久稳定？ 第三，当面对极硬的弹簧（Stiff ODEs）时，为什么连四阶龙格-库塔（RK4）都会崩溃，唯独后向隐式欧拉 (Backward Implicit Euler) 能稳如泰山？ Every physical simulation—sparks, cloth, fluids, orbital mechanics—is governed by Newton's Second Law $\mathbf{F} = m\ddot{\mathbf{x}}$ stepped across discrete time intervals $\Delta t = h$. Three profound questions arise immediately: First, why does the intuitive Forward Explicit Euler method multiply the energy of a simple undamped spring by $(1 + h^2)$ on every single step, exploding to infinity? Second, why does swapping just two lines of code (updating velocity before position: Symplectic Euler) make the transition matrix determinant identically $\det(A) \equiv 1$, stabilizing the orbit forever? Third, for ultra-stiff springs, why does even 4th-Order Runge–Kutta (RK4) blow up while Backward Implicit Euler survives unconditionally?

二阶 ODE 降阶为相空间一阶向量场 & 显式/隐式欧拉的勾股定理证明 Phase-Space Order Reduction & Pythagorean Proof of Explicit Explosion vs. Implicit Damping

MIT 6.837 L07：为什么显式欧拉走的是直角三角形的斜边，而隐式欧拉走的是直角边？ MIT 6.837 L07: Why Explicit Euler steps onto the hypotenuse while Implicit Euler steps onto the leg

显式欧拉 (Explicit Euler)：切线垂直于半径 $\implies$ 斜边必大于直角边！ Explicit Euler: Tangent $\perp$ Radius $\implies$ Hypotenuse > Radius!

将位置 $x$ 和速度 $v = \dot{x}$ 拼成相空间状态向量 $\mathbf{Y} = [x, v]^T$。对于单位角频率简谐弹簧 $\ddot{x} = -x$，一阶微分方程为 $\dot{\mathbf{Y}} = [v, -x]^T$。 注意 $\mathbf{Y}\cdot\dot{\mathbf{Y}} = xv - xv = 0$，即导数向量场处处与半径垂直！ 前向显式欧拉沿起点 $\mathbf{Y}_n$ 的切线走一步 $h$： Combine position $x$ and velocity $v = \dot{x}$ into the phase-space state $\mathbf{Y} = [x, v]^T$. For a harmonic oscillator $\ddot{x} = -x$, $\dot{\mathbf{Y}} = [v, -x]^T$, which is strictly perpendicular to $\mathbf{Y}$ ($\mathbf{Y}\cdot\dot{\mathbf{Y}} = 0$). Explicit Euler steps along the tangent at $\mathbf{Y}_n$:

$$\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} = \mathbf{Y}_n + h\dot{\mathbf{Y}}_n = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & h \\ -h & 1 \end{bmatrix}\mathbf{Y}_n \implies \|\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}\| = \sqrt{1 + h^2}\,\|\mathbf{Y}_n\|$$

💥 几何判据：在直角三角形中，新状态 $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}$ 是斜边！每走一步，系统总能量 $E \propto \|\mathbf{Y}\|^2$ 严格膨胀 $(1 + h^2)$ 倍，形成向外发散的死亡螺旋！ 💥 Geometric Verdict: $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}$ is the hypotenuse of a right triangle! Every step multiplies total energy $E \propto \|\mathbf{Y}\|^2$ by $(1 + h^2) > 1$, spiraling out to infinity!

后向隐式欧拉 (Implicit Euler)：用终点斜率倒推 $\implies$ 直角边必小于斜边！ Backward Implicit Euler: Endpoint Tangent $\implies$ Leg < Hypotenuse!

后向隐式欧拉改用下一步未知终点 $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}$ 处的切向量来更新：$\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} = \mathbf{Y}_n + h f(\mathbf{Y}_{n+1})$。移项求解线性方程组： Backward Implicit Euler evaluates the derivative at the unknown future state $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}$: $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} = \mathbf{Y}_n + h f(\mathbf{Y}_{n+1})$. Solving the linear system gives:

$$\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -h \\ h & 1 \end{bmatrix}^{-1}\mathbf{Y}_n = \frac{1}{1+h^2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & h \\ -h & 1 \end{bmatrix}\mathbf{Y}_n \implies \|\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}\| = \frac{\|\mathbf{Y}_n\|}{\sqrt{1+h^2}}$$

🛡️ 几何判据：由于 $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} \perp h f(\mathbf{Y}_{n+1})$ 且二者之和等于 $\mathbf{Y}_n$，现在旧状态 $\mathbf{Y}_n$ 成了斜边，新状态 $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}$ 成了直角边！因此不论 $h$ 多大，模长永远收缩 $\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+h^2}} < 1$（无条件稳定，但带数值阻尼）。 🛡️ Geometric Verdict: Since $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} \perp h f(\mathbf{Y}_{n+1})$ and they sum to $\mathbf{Y}_n$, the old state $\mathbf{Y}_n$ is the hypotenuse and $\mathbf{Y}_{n+1}$ is a leg! Energy shrinks by $\frac{1}{1+h^2} < 1$ for any $h > 0$!

为什么只调换一行代码顺序，行列式就从 $1+h^2$ 变成了严格的 $1$？ Why swapping two lines of code changes the determinant from $1+h^2$ to exactly $1$

Symplectic Euler • $\det A = 1$

半隐式/辛欧拉：先算新速度，立刻更新位置 Symplectic Euler: Velocity First, Then Position

先用旧位置算 $v_{n+1} = v_n - h x_n$，再用新速度算 $x_{n+1} = x_n + h v_{n+1} = (1-h^2)x_n + h v_n$。这是两个行列式为 $1$ 的水平/垂直剪切矩阵（Shear Matrices）的乘积： Update $v_{n+1} = v_n - h x_n$ first, then use $v_{n+1}$ immediately for $x_{n+1} = x_n + h v_{n+1}$. This factors into two unit-determinant shear matrices:

$$A_{\text{symp}} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & h \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -h & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1-h^2 & h \\ -h & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$ $$\det(A_{\text{symp}}) = (1-h^2) + h^2 \equiv \mathbf{1}!$$
2nd-Order • Midpoint Method

二阶中点法 (Midpoint / RK2) Midpoint Method (RK2, $O(h^2)$)

既然起点斜率偏外、终点斜率偏内，那我们就先试探性走半步 $h/2$ 到时间中点，采样中点处的切向量来走完整一步，消去一阶泰勒误差项： Since the start tangent overshoots and the end tangent undershoots, probe halfway to $t + h/2$ first, then use the midpoint slope for the full step:

$$\mathbf{Y}_{\text{mid}} = \mathbf{Y}_n + \frac{h}{2}f(\mathbf{Y}_n)$$ $$\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} = \mathbf{Y}_n + h\, f(\mathbf{Y}_{\text{mid}})$$
Gold Standard • 4th-Order RK4

四阶龙格-库塔 (Runge–Kutta RK4) 4th-Order Runge–Kutta (RK4, $O(h^4)$)

在一步之内巧妙探测 4 个斜率（起点 $\mathbf{k}_1$、两次半步中点校正 $\mathbf{k}_2, \mathbf{k}_3$、终点 $\mathbf{k}_4$），按辛普森积分法则 $1:2:2:1$ 加权平均，泰勒展开一直精确匹配到 $h^4$ 项： Evaluates 4 slopes per step ($\mathbf{k}_1$ at start, $\mathbf{k}_2,\mathbf{k}_3$ at midpoints, $\mathbf{k}_4$ at end) weighted by Simpson's rule $1:2:2:1$, matching the Taylor series through $h^4$:

$$\mathbf{k}_1 = f(\mathbf{Y}_n), \quad \mathbf{k}_2 = f(\mathbf{Y}_n + \tfrac{h}{2}\mathbf{k}_1)$$ $$\mathbf{k}_3 = f(\mathbf{Y}_n + \tfrac{h}{2}\mathbf{k}_2), \quad \mathbf{k}_4 = f(\mathbf{Y}_n + h\mathbf{k}_3)$$ $$\mathbf{Y}_{n+1} = \mathbf{Y}_n + \frac{h}{6}(\mathbf{k}_1 + 2\mathbf{k}_2 + 2\mathbf{k}_3 + \mathbf{k}_4)$$
